# Cinder Wallet Terms of use

Last updated: 9 September 2026

The published copy is `docs/legal/terms.html` (GitHub Pages) and the same file ships in the extension
as `legal/terms.html` from `public/legal/terms.html`; `node scripts/sync-legal.mjs` keeps the two in step.

Cinder Wallet is self-custodial software. You control the keys. Blockchain transactions are irreversible.

## Risk

- Do not store funds you cannot afford to lose
- Mainnet SOL and tokens have real value. Devnet does not
- Phishing sites can ask you to sign harmful transactions. Read every approval
- If you lose the seed phrase or password, nobody can restore the wallet

## No warranty

The software is provided as-is, without warranty. It is a portfolio implementation of a Solana wallet, not a bank, broker, or custodian.

## Acceptable use

Use the extension only with accounts you control. Do not use it to commit crimes. You are responsible for taxes and for complying with laws that apply to you.
